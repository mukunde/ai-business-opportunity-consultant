# UX/UI Design v1

## Purpose

Define the user experience and interface principles for the AI Business Opportunity Consultant.

## Target Users

- AI labs
- Innovation teams
- Digital transformation teams
- Business stakeholders submitting AI opportunity ideas

## Core Screens

- Opportunity intake
- Adaptive interview
- Context completeness review
- Scoring and recommendation
- Executive summary
- Opportunity history

## Design Principles

- Make uncertainty visible.
- Keep business users oriented during the interview.
- Separate facts, assumptions, and unknowns.
- Present recommendations with confidence and rationale.
